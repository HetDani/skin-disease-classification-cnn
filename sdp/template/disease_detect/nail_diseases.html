What is Nail Diseases?

Nail diseases are conditions that affect the appearance, texture, or strength of the nails. Symptoms of nail diseases can include changes in nail color, thickness, shape, or texture, as well as pain or discomfort around the nail bed.

Symptoms

The symptoms of nail diseases can vary depending on the underlying condition. Some common symptoms include:


  • Discoloration of the nail
  • Thickening or thinning of the nail
  • Changes in nail shape or texture
  • Separation of the nail from the nail bed
  • Pain or discomfort around the nail bed

When to See a Doctor

If you notice any changes in the appearance or texture of your nails, or if you experience pain or discomfort around the nail bed, it is important to see a healthcare provider or dermatologist for an evaluation.

How to Treat Nail Diseases

Treatment for nail diseases depends on the underlying condition. Options may include topical or oral medications, nail removal, or laser therapy. Common treatments for nail diseases include antifungal medications for fungal infections, oral antibiotics for bacterial infections, or corticosteroid injections for inflammatory conditions.
