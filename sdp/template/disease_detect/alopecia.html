What is Alopecia?

Alopecia is a medical condition that results in hair loss on the scalp, face, or other areas of the body. Androgenetic alopecia is the most common form of hair loss, which affects both men and women and is caused by genetic and hormonal factors.

Symptoms

  • Gradual hair thinning (androgenetic alopecia)
  • Round, coin-sized patches of hair loss (alopecia areata)
  • Overall hair thinning and increased shedding (telogen effluvium)

When to see a doctor

If you are experiencing hair loss or thinning, it is recommended to see a healthcare provider or dermatologist for a proper diagnosis and treatment plan. Early diagnosis and treatment can help prevent further hair loss and improve the chances of hair regrowth.

How to treat Alopecia

  • Medications such as minoxidil and finasteride (androgenetic alopecia)
  • Hair transplant surgery (androgenetic alopecia)
  • Corticosteroids and immunomodulating drugs (alopecia areata)
  • Addressing underlying physical or emotional stress (telogen effluvium)
